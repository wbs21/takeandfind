Quick start

Save your first memory

  1. Tap Take.
  2. Take a photo of the item.
  3. Tap OK to use the photo.
  4. Tap the microphone and say where the item is.
  5. Tap the microphone again to stop.
  6. Listen to the recording, check What we heard, and tap Save.

To find it later, tap Find, ask a question, and look for the matching photo. Tap Play to hear your own voice.

Permissions

Camera, microphone, and speech recognition

Take&Find asks for each permission when it is needed. If access was denied:

  1. Open the iPhone or iPad Settings app.
  2. Scroll to Take&Find.
  3. Turn on Camera, Microphone, and Speech Recognition.
  4. Return to Take&Find and try again.
If the microphone is off, the recording page shows Open Settings and Try again.

Recording

Short voice notes, up to one minute

Tap the microphone once to start and again to stop. The last ten seconds show a countdown. At one minute, Take&Find stops automatically and keeps the recording ready to review.

Use Play recording to check the sound before saving. Use Re-record to replace it.

Search

Ask in your own words

Tap Find and ask a question such as “Where are my reading glasses?” You can also use Type it.

Best match means the strongest match found. Closest match means Take&Find is not certain, so check the photo carefully. Every matching result has a Play action when a recording is available.

Recognized words

Correct the words, not the voice

Speech recognition may miss a name or location. Tap Fix words, type the correction, and save. Your original recording remains unchanged.

If speech recognition is unavailable, the photo and recording can still be saved. Use Fix words to add searchable words.

Backup

Keep a copy you control

Take&Find stores memories locally. You can use the iPhone backup setting or create an encrypted backup file from Settings.

The encrypted backup password cannot be recovered. Keep it somewhere safe. A backup file contains both the database and media.

Purchase

$6.99 once, not a subscription

Take&Find includes 10 free memories. One $6.99 purchase unlocks unlimited memories.

To restore a purchase, open Take&Find Settings → Plan → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID that made the original purchase.

Privacy and data

Your memories stay on your device

Take&Find does not require an account and does not upload your photos, recordings, notes, or search index. You can archive a memory or erase app data from the available settings.
