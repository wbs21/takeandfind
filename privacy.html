Privacy Policy

Your memories stay yours.

Take&Find is designed to work without an account, ads, or tracking.

Effective date: November 25, 2026

On this page Summary What is stored On-device processing Data collection Backups Purchases This website Your choices Children Medical disclaimer Changes Contact

Summary

  • No account is required.
  • No ads and no tracking.
  • Photos, recordings, notes, and search indexes stay on your device.
  • Speech and text recognition run on the device.
  • You control optional backups.

Information stored by the app

Take&Find stores the information you choose to save, including:

  • Photos you take or sample photos used in the app.
  • Voice recordings, up to one minute each.
  • Recognized voice and photo text used for search.
  • Notes, item type, timestamps, and archive status.
  • App preferences, such as text size and backup settings.

This information is stored in the app’s local storage on your iPhone or iPad.

On-device processing

Take&Find uses Apple Speech and Vision frameworks for speech recognition and text recognition. These features process data on the device. Take&Find does not send photos, recordings, recognized text, or search queries to a Take&Find server.

Apple may provide system speech or text-recognition assets. Their handling is governed by Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

Data collection and tracking

Take&Find does not require an account. It does not include advertising, third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or cross-app tracking. The app does not sell personal information.

The App Store privacy label states that the developer does not collect data through the app.

Backups

Take&Find may be included in device backups when the user enables that setting. The app also offers an encrypted backup file that the user creates and stores through the system file picker or iCloud Drive.

Encrypted backup files are protected with a password chosen by the user. The password cannot be recovered by Take&Find. Files stored in iCloud Drive or other services are also subject to those services’ terms.

Purchases and Family Sharing

The one-time purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. Take&Find does not receive or store your payment card details. Family Sharing shares the purchase entitlement through Apple; it does not share your saved photos or recordings.

This website

This static website does not use advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or cookies controlled by Take&Find. The hosting provider may process standard technical request logs, such as IP address, browser type, requested page, and time, to deliver and secure the site.

Your choices

  • Archive individual memories from the detail screen.
  • Turn device backup inclusion on or off in Settings.
  • Create an encrypted backup you control.
  • Use For family to restore archived memories or erase app data.
  • Change camera, microphone, speech, or photos permissions in iOS Settings.

Children

Take&Find is a general-purpose memory aid and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children because it does not operate an account or data-collection service.

Medical disclaimer

Take&Find is not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnose a condition, recommend treatment, or replace emergency or medical care. Always follow the instructions from a qualified healthcare professional.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date will be revised. Material changes will be reflected before the affected feature becomes available.
